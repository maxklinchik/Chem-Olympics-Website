When Fertilizer Nitrogen Leaves the Field

Nitrate pollution is a common and simple form of chemical waste. It starts with everyday fertilizers, moves through runoff and groundwater, and can trigger algal blooms, oxygen loss, and health risks before it finally reaches rivers, lakes, and drinking water systems.

30-50% of applied nitrogen can be lost to runoff, leaching, or gaseous escape
10 mg/L U.S. drinking water limit for nitrate-nitrogen
400+ coastal and inland hypoxic zones documented worldwide
2.7B kg rough scale of nitrogen fertilizer used globally each year
